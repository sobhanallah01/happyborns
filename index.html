Why we're really doing this

We're deleting the distance
between people.

Borders, languages, flags, beliefs, the color of our skin — we let them convince us that strangers are far away. They aren't. The distance was never real.

Your birthday is the most neutral thing about you. You didn't earn it, choose it, or fight for it. It belongs to no country and no religion. It's pure chance — and by pure chance, it's shared by millions of people who look nothing like you and live nowhere near you.

So we use that one honest coincidence to put a student in Cairo, a nurse in Brazil, and a farmer in Japan in the same room, as equals — a CEO in Malaysia, an office worker in Kenya, a refugee in Canada, an entrepreneur in Algeria, a teacher in Mexico, a grandmother in Poland, all sharing a single square on the calendar. Not to sell anything. Just to prove they were never really strangers.

  • There's no app yet — just small group chats to start.
  • Your birthday room might take a few weeks to fill up.
  • If there isn't enough interest, we'll tell you honestly.

Register only if you're genuinely curious. We'd rather have 100 real people than 10,000 ghosts.
